# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Startup teams coordinating product delivery across projects, tasks, and teammates. They work quickly, switch context often, and need to understand ownership, status, and the next useful action without navigating through administrative clutter.

## Product Purpose

Give startup teams a dependable shared workspace for planning projects, moving tasks through delivery, and keeping day-to-day execution visible. Success means a teammate can sign in, understand current work within seconds, create or update work without friction, and trust that every action is saved.

## Brand Personality

Fast, technical, and focused. The interface should feel precise and quietly confident, taking inspiration from Linear's density, keyboard-minded efficiency, and disciplined hierarchy without copying its visual identity.

## Anti-references

Avoid playful multicolor task-board styling, heavy enterprise administration, decorative glass effects, oversized rounded cards, and generic dashboard grids. The product should not resemble a marketing landing page or bury operational work beneath ornamental metrics.

## Design Principles

1. Put current work before reporting: tasks, blockers, ownership, and next actions lead each screen.
2. Make speed visible: interactions feel immediate, navigation stays predictable, and common actions remain close to their context.
3. Earn every surface: use hierarchy, whitespace, and dividers before adding containers or cards.
4. Keep system state honest: loading, empty, error, saving, and success states explain what is happening and how to recover.
5. Scale through consistency: shared patterns and language should work for a two-person startup and a growing multi-team workspace.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic controls, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, and status indicators that never rely on color alone. Layouts must remain usable at 320px width, with zoomed text, and across light and dark themes.
